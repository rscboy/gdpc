Original Questionnaire Letter

The full letter sent to candidates is included below for transparency and public reference.

View full letter text

The Greater Dayton Peace Coalition (GDPC) is a diverse, nonpartisan group of activists united by a commitment to peace and equity in the Greater Dayton area. You are a candidate running in the 2026 general election for Ohio’s 10th congressional district. We ask that you share your positions on foreign relations, military spending, and the promotion of our region as a hub for military-related technology and manufacturing.

Please answer the following questions honestly and in keeping with your own values. Some require only a yes or no; you are welcome to elaborate as needed.

  1. Do you believe Israel should be held to the same standards as other countries regarding nuclear weapons?
  2. Do you consider Palestinians to be living under a system of apartheid?
  3. Do you consider the situation in Gaza to be a genocide?
  4. Will you vote to provide continued financial and military support to Israel considering their actions over the last few years?
  5. Will you accept campaign contributions from AIPAC or related entities?
  6. Do you support the embargo on Cuba?
  7. Do you believe money currently spent on the U.S. military and military aid abroad could be better spent improving the lives of citizens in the 10th congressional district?
  8. Do you believe the 10th congressional district would benefit from diversifying employment opportunities to be less reliant on military-adjacent industries?

Thank you for your time. We hope your responses give us a clearer picture of your candidacy and the values that will guide your representation of our district.

Sincerely,
